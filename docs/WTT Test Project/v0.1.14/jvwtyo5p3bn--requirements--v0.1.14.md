# Project Requirements Document

## Executive Summary
This document outlines the requirements for a simulation system designed to enhance the educational experience of chemistry students and instructors. The system aims to provide an interactive platform for students to learn about chemical processes, safety protocols, and the operational aspects of chemical processing in a controlled environment. 

## User Stories
The following user stories capture the key perspectives and needs of the primary stakeholders:

1. **User Story ID: US-1**
   - **Description:** As a chemistry student, I want to simulate the process of submitting mixtures into a control environment so that I can enhance my understanding of chemical processes.

2. **User Story ID: US-2**
   - **Description:** As a chemistry student, I want to learn about safety protocols in chemical processing so that I can operate safely in a lab environment.

3. **User Story ID: US-3**
   - **Description:** As a teacher, I want to assess students' understanding of chemical processes through simulation results so that I can provide feedback and additional guidance.

## Business Rules
The following business rules define the operational constraints that must be adhered to within the simulation system:

1. **Business Rule ID: BR-1**
   - **Description:** The temperature of the tank must be maintained between 35-40 ºC for optimal enzyme function.

2. **Business Rule ID: BR-2**
   - **Description:** If the cooling jacket valve is closed and the temperature trends upwards, it must be opened to maintain appropriate conditions.

3. **Business Rule ID: BR-3**
   - **Description:** In the event of a substrate flow sensor failure, students must notify a supervisor and may take action to turn off the pump and valve.

## Functional Requirements
The following functional requirements specify the capabilities that the simulation system must provide:

1. **Functional Requirement ID: FR-1**
   - **Description:** The system must allow students to simulate filling the tank with 1100 liters of substrate mix and 50 liters of enzyme.

2. **Functional Requirement ID: FR-2**
   - **Description:** The simulation must present scenarios including normal operation, trending out of specification, and hazardous situations.

3. **Functional Requirement ID: FR-3**
   - **Description:** The system must prompt students with questions regarding their observations and decisions in response to simulated events.

## Compliance Requirements
The following compliance requirements ensure that the simulation system operates within safe parameters:

1. **Compliance Requirement ID: CR-1**
   - **Description:** The simulation must operate in a safe, controlled environment, preventing any actual chemical reactions.

## Quality Assurance Requirements
The following quality assurance requirements outline the expectations for user experience:

1. **Quality Assurance Requirement ID: QA-1**
   - **Description:** The simulation's user interface must be intuitive and easy to navigate for students.

2. **Quality Assurance Requirement ID: QA-2**
   - **Description:** The system must minimize lag or downtime during simulations to ensure a seamless learning experience.

## Conclusion
This Project Requirements Document serves as a foundational resource for the development of the chemistry simulation system. The outlined user stories, business rules, functional, compliance, and quality assurance requirements will guide the project team in delivering a robust and effective educational tool that meets the needs of both students and instructors in the field of chemistry.
