# Requirements

## Executive Summary
This document outlines the project requirements for a chemical simulation system designed for educational purposes. It includes user stories, business rules, functional requirements, non-functional requirements, and quality assurance criteria. The goal is to provide a safe and effective learning environment for chemistry students and educators.

## User Stories
The following user stories capture the needs and objectives of the primary stakeholders:

1. **User Story US-1**  
   As a chemistry student, I want to simulate the process of submitting mixtures into a controlled environment so that I can enhance my understanding of chemical processes.

2. **User Story US-2**  
   As a chemistry student, I want to learn about safety protocols in chemical processing so that I can operate safely in a lab environment.

3. **User Story US-3**  
   As a teacher, I want to assess students' understanding of chemical processes through simulation results so that I can provide feedback and additional guidance.

## Business Rules
The following business rules must be adhered to during the operation of the simulation system:

1. **Business Rule BR-1**  
   The temperature of the tank must be maintained between 35-40 ºC for optimal enzyme function.

2. **Business Rule BR-2**  
   If the cooling jacket valve is closed and temperature trends upwards, it must be opened to maintain appropriate conditions.

3. **Business Rule BR-3**  
   In the event of a substrate flow sensor failure, students must notify a supervisor and are authorized to turn off the pump and valve.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements
The system must meet the following functional requirements:

1. **Functional Requirement FR-1**  
   The system must allow students to simulate filling the tank with 1,100 liters of substrate mix and 50 liters of enzyme.

2. **Functional Requirement FR-2**  
   The simulation must present scenarios that include normal operation, trending out of specification, and potentially dangerous situations.

3. **Functional Requirement FR-3**  
   The system must prompt students with questions regarding their observations and decisions in response to simulated events.

### Constraints Requirements
The following constraints must be observed:

1. **Constraint Requirement CR-1**  
   The simulation must operate in a safe, controlled environment, preventing any actual chemical reactions.

### Quality Assurance Requirements
To ensure a high-quality user experience, the following quality assurance requirements are established:

1. **Quality Assurance Requirement QA-1**  
   The simulation's user interface must be intuitive and easy to navigate for students.

2. **Quality Assurance Requirement QA-2**  
   The system must minimize lag or downtime during simulations to ensure a seamless learning experience.

## Conclusion
This Requirements Document serves as a comprehensive guide for the development of a chemical simulation system aimed at enhancing educational outcomes for chemistry students and teachers. By adhering to the outlined user stories, business rules, functional and quality assurance requirements, the project will ensure a safe and effective learning environment that fosters a deeper understanding of chemical processes.
